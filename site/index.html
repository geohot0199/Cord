04 — Setup

Download, add your key, say it.

Five steps, about four minutes. Each one shows the screen it happens on.

  1. 1

    Download and install

    Get the Nūs Windows installer. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway — it isn’t code-signed yet.

  2. 2

    Add your AI key

    Under Settings → AI provider, paste a Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI or Nvidia key. Keys are encrypted with your OS keychain and never shown again.

  3. 3

    Give it your stuff

    Under Folders, add your notes, docs and calendar: PDF, Word, text, Markdown and calendar files. It answers from them when they help.

  4. 4

    Say it, it acts

    Hold CtrlAlt and say “open Notepad, type hello”. Say a few in a row and it offers to keep them as a routine.

  5. 5

    Point at anything

    Press CtrlShiftT on a button, an error or a slide. The Knot’s thread runs to that exact spot, and the answer lands right there.

06 — Questions

14 questions

Is it free?

Yes, to start. Nūs Companion is a free download with no account required to install. Answers come from your own AI key — Gemini and Nvidia both offer free keys — or from a local endpoint you run yourself. Nūs charges nothing for the app and takes no cut of your model usage.

Is it local? What leaves my computer?

Your notes, routines, transcripts and keys stay on your PC. A question, and the screenshot that goes with it, goes only to the AI provider you configured — straight to them, or through your own endpoint. There is no Nūs server in between. Folders you turn on share short excerpts the same way, and only when they help answer. Usage stats are anonymous and can be switched off in Settings.

Do I need an AI key?

For answers, yes — one provider is enough. The back of the app ships with adapters for Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenRouter, a university gateway, vLLM, Ollama), plus Claude Code if it is already signed in on your machine. Simple asks like “open YouTube” or “a bit quieter” never touch a model. Speech to text runs locally, so it needs no key at all.

Is it only for students?

No. Students are where Nūs started, and the semester map is still here. Nūs Companion works for anyone on Windows, and the bigger vision is one companion across school, work and life.

What happened to Nūs for Students?

It has moved into Nūs Companion, and that is the one download now. If you already have Nūs for Students, it still opens and your semester stays on your computer, but it gets no more updates. Everything new ships in the Companion.

Why does Windows warn me when I install?

The installers aren’t code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen asks first. Choose More info, then Run anyway. A signed installer is on the roadmap.

It won’t install on Windows. What now?

Check you ran Nus-Setup.exe, not the Portable build or a .yml file. If your antivirus took it, restore it from quarantine and run it again. If there is no Run anyway button, Smart App Control or S mode is blocking unsigned apps. It installs in a few seconds with no questions, so look for Nūs in the Start menu. Still stuck? Tell us what you saw.

Is there a Mac version?

Yes, as a beta. Nūs Companion runs on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer); Intel Macs are not supported yet. Download it under download, where the first-open steps are too. Hands and updates inside the app are not on Mac yet.

My Mac says Nūs can’t be opened or is damaged.

macOS stops the first open because Nūs is not notarised yet. Drag Nūs into Applications first. If it says Apple could not verify it: click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. If it says it is damaged: click Cancel, open Terminal, type xattr -cr with a space at the end, drag Nūs from Applications into the window, and press Return.

What can the voice actions actually do?

The reflex model picks one action from a fixed set: open an app, focus a window, search, adjust volume, type a line, and more. Anything that leaves your machine passes through a preview first. When there is no matching action, the request goes to your answering model instead of guessing.

What happens when it mishears?

There is no undo on the screen yet, so you fix it by hand. You can also type a command instead of saying it — every voice action has a typed twin.

Is what I saw in the videos real?

The voice actions, pointing and routines in our videos are in the released Companion. Anything not shipped yet is labelled “where it’s heading”, here and in the app.

Is it open source?

The older Nūs for Students app (Nūs Desktop) is open source on GitHub — this repository. It is MIT licensed, and the desktop, Android and Companion code paths are all auditable.

What does it need on my machine?

Windows 10 or 11, about 200 MB of disk, and a microphone if you want to talk. The model runs on the provider’s side, so there is no GPU requirement. Speech to text runs on your own CPU with whisper.cpp.
